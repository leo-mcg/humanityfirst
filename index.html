Who we are

Serving humanity beyond borders

Humanity First is a global humanitarian organization dedicated to protecting human life and dignity. Founded in 1995 in the UK, and registered as a charity in Canada in 2004, it is a non-political, non-religious international disaster relief and development agency.

We provide emergency relief, sustainable development programs, and capacity-building initiatives, guided by compassion, dignity, and the principle of putting humanity first.

  • Non-political & non-religious

    Aid is delivered purely on the basis of humanitarian need, regardless of race, creed, religion, or political affiliation.

  • Global scale, local roots

    Relief overseas and a Feed a Family Food Bank in Canada, serving low-income families since 2008.

  • Compassion & dignity

    Guided by compassion, dignity, and the principle of putting humanity first.

The Masroor Centre for Healthcare is open

We have officially opened the doors of the Masroor Centre for Healthcare, Ivory Coast Hospital. A moment made possible by you. We wanted to say thank you!

In Yopougon-Gesco near Abidjan, Humanity First has built a modern hospital featuring emergency care, maternity suites, neonatal care, ICU, operating theatres, diagnostics, pharmacy, laboratory and more. Construction is complete, and donations are now urgently needed for hospital operations.
